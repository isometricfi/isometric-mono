# History

The **History** page provides a complete record of your trading activity on Isometric.

## Trade History

In the Trade History section, you can review all your past transactions, including:

- **Filled Offers**: Offers you wrote that were accepted by buyers.
- **Bought Options**: Options you purchased.
- **Settlement Status**: Whether an option expired In-the-Money (ITM) or Out-of-the-Money (OTM).

### Understanding Statuses

- **Settled**: The option has reached its expiry and funds have been distributed.
- **Cancelled**: An offer you created was withdrawn before being accepted.
- **Expired**: An offer reached its validity date without being filled.

## Transaction Logs

Isometric provides a transparent log of all on-chain events related to your account:

1. **Premiums Received**: Income earned from writing options.
2. **Premiums Paid**: Cost of buying options.
3. **Payouts**: Funds received from profitable (ITM) options.
4. **Collateral Returns**: BTC returned to your available balance after an option expires.

## Exporting Data

You can use the data on the History page to help with tax reporting or performance analysis. Every transaction includes a unique ID that can be verified on-chain.
