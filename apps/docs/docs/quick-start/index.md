# Overview

Welcome! This guide will help you get started with Isometric, whether you want to **write options** (earn premiums) or **buy options** (gain leveraged exposure).

## Choose Your Path

### I Want to Write Options

**Option writers** (also called sellers) earn premium income by offering covered calls on their Bitcoin holdings.

**What you'll do:**
1. Deposit BTC as collateral
2. Create an offer specifying strike price, premium, and expiry
3. Collect premiums when buyers accept your offer
4. Keep your collateral if the option expires out-of-the-money

**Best for:** BTC holders who want to generate income from their holdings

**[Start Writing Options →](/quick-start/write)**

---

### I Want to Buy Options

**Option buyers** pay a premium upfront to gain the right (but not obligation) to profit if Bitcoin rises above a certain price (the strike price).

**What you'll do:**
1. Deposit BTC to cover the premium
2. Browse available offers and select strike/expiry
3. Pay the premium to lock in your position
4. Receive profits automatically if the option expires in-the-money

**Best for:** Traders seeking leveraged exposure to Bitcoin price movements

**[Start Buying Options →](/quick-start/buy)**
