# Account Setup

Before you can trade options on Isometric, you'll need to create an account and deposit funds. This guide walks you through the process.

## Prerequisites

- A Bitcoin wallet 
- Some BTC for deposits 

## Step 1: Connect Your Wallet

1. Visit the Isometric web app
2. Click **"Connect Wallet"** in the top right
3. Select your wallet provider from the list
4. Approve the connection request in your wallet

:::tip
Isometric uses Bitcoin address signatures for authentication. You'll sign messages to prove ownership of your address without exposing your private keys.
:::

## Step 2: Create Your Account

Once your wallet is connected:

1. You'll be prompted to **create a profile**
2. Sign the account creation message in your wallet
3. Your account is now created!

## Step 3: Choose Deposit Method

To fund your account, open the account panel on the top right and click **"Deposit"**. You have two options:

### Deposit with Connected Wallet
1. Select **"Wallet"**
2. Enter the amount of BTC you wish to deposit
3. Confirm the transaction in your wallet

### Send to Deposit Address
If you want to send BTC from an external wallet or exchange:
1. Select **"Deposit Address"**
1. Your unique deposit address is displayed at the bottom
2. Copy this address or scan the QR code
3. Send BTC to this address from your external wallet

:::warning Important
**Minimum Deposit Amount: ₿0.0005**
Deposits below this amount may not be credited. Each user has a unique deposit address; always verify you're sending to YOUR address.
:::

## Step 4: Wait for Confirmation

Once you've sent the transaction:

1. The Bitcoin network needs to confirm the transaction (6 to 7 confirmations)
2. **Automatic Detection**: We automatically credit your account once processed.

### How Long Does It Take?

- About 60-70 minutes (depending on network activity)


## Available Balance

Your balance is split into:
- **Available**: Funds you can use to write or buy options
- **Locked**: Funds currently tied up in active positions
