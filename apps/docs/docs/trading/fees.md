# Fees

Two fees. Both shown in the app before you sign.

## Writer side

When a buyer accepts your offer, the platform takes a small percentage of the premium. You receive the rest, credited immediately to your available balance.

Example: a buyer accepts your offer at a 2% premium on 1 BTC. They pay 0.02 BTC. After the fee, you receive 0.019 BTC.

## Buyer side

When an option expires in the money, the platform takes a percentage of your gross profit before paying out. If the option expires worthless, no fee applies because there's no profit.

Example: gross profit of 0.01 BTC at expiry. After the fee, you receive 0.008 BTC.

## That's it

No spreads. No fees on cancellations. No fees on losses. The Bitcoin network fee on withdrawals is separate.
