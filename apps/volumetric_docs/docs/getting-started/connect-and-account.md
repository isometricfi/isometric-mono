# Connect an account

Isometric uses your existing BTC wallet for signing. There's no separate password to remember.

## Connecting a wallet

1. Open the app and click **Connect**.
2. Pick your wallet and approve the connection.
3. The app sets up your Isometric account.

## Setting up your account

The first time you connect, the app asks you to sign a message to prove the account belongs to you.

## Username

Pick a username from the **Account** panel. The username is public and appears on shared portfolio and trade links. You can change it at any time.

## Invite codes

If a friend referred you, paste their invite code during setup. You can find your own invite link in the Account panel under **Invite users** once your account is live.
