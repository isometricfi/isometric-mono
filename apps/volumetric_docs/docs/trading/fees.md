# Fees

Two fees. Both shown in the app before you sign.

| Side | Fee | When it's charged |
|---|---|---|
| Writer | **5%** of premium | When a buyer accepts. You receive the rest immediately. |
| Buyer | **20%** of profit | Only on options that expire in the money. No profit, no fee. |

**Writer example:** buyer accepts at a 2% premium on 1 BTC (0.02 BTC). After the 5% fee, you receive 0.019 BTC.

**Buyer example:** gross profit of 0.01 BTC at expiry. After the 20% fee, you receive 0.008 BTC.

No spreads. No fees on cancellations. No fees on losses. The Bitcoin network fee on withdrawals is separate.
