# History

Every settled trade you've been part of, as buyer or writer.

Each row shows date, role, type (call), size, strike, earnings/amount paid, settlement price, final payout, and result (profit, loss, or breakeven).

## Stats

The header shows aggregate stats:

- **PnL**: total profit and loss in BTC.
- **Win rate**: percentage of profitable trades.
- **Volume**: total BTC traded.
- **Trades**: number of settled trades.

## Filtering and sharing

Filter by date range, role, and result. Use **Share** to share your trading stats publicly via a link or image.
