# Portfolio

Your active positions in two tabs.

## Active

Every option you currently hold, on either side of the trade. Cards show the current stats (PnL, time remaining, strike, your role) and update in real time.

When an option expires and settles, it leaves Active and appears in [History](./history) with the final result.

## Offers

Offers you've written that haven't been accepted yet. Cards show the offer's parameters and how much of it has filled.

You can cancel any unfilled portion at any time. Cancelling has no effect on already-accepted portions; those continue as active options until expiry.
